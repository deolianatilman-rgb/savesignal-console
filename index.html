The Problem

Disaster-prone regions face two gaps.

Regions like the Philippines sit at the intersection of two unsolved problems: a warning gap, where instruments alone can't give enough lead time before a quake, and a communication gap, where a single storm can take down every cell tower survivors would otherwise call for help on. Most solutions only ever address one side of that timeline.

The Approach

Two phases, one device.

Phase 01 — Pre-Disaster

Prediction

Existing pet-wearable sensors and government hazard data are fused into one AI model that issues an early warning before conventional instruments alone would.

  • ✓ Wearable vitals + behavior tracking via existing collars
  • ✓ AI behavior classification layered on sensor data
  • ✓ Fused with PAGASA meteorological / seismic feeds
Phase 02 — Post-Disaster

Mesh Rescue

When towers collapse, every phone, wearable, and collar in the field becomes a relay node — hopping an SOS signal until it reaches a rescue team.

  • ✓ BLE + LoRa hop-by-hop relay, no towers required
  • ✓ Trained & domestic dogs both extend mesh coverage
  • ✓ Self-healing routing around offline devices
