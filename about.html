About Us

We are :4-ever.

A youth team building for the Korea-ASEAN Youth Diplomacy Program — pairing Korean pet-tech and AI expertise with ASEAN-side disaster infrastructure, so early warning and rescue don't depend on hardware that doesn't exist yet.

Why :4-ever

Built on the idea that connection shouldn't have an expiry date.

Most disaster tech stops working the moment the network does. We named ourselves :4-ever because the mesh is designed to keep relaying a signal for as long as a single device survives — no subscription, no tower, no expiry condition on someone's chance of being found.

01

Build on what exists

Korean pet-wearables, government sensor feeds, and off-the-shelf phones are already in the field. SaveSignal is an intelligence layer on top of them, not a new device to manufacture and distribute.

02

Design for zero connectivity

We designed for the case where there is no network to fall back on at all — not degraded bandwidth, but a fully collapsed tower grid. Everything routes device-to-device from there.

03

Every device counts

A rescuer's phone, a hiker's watch, a family dog's collar — each one running mesh-AI adds another possible hop. Coverage grows with the number of ordinary devices nearby, not with new infrastructure spend.

04

Korea-ASEAN partnership

Korean hardware and AI expertise paired with ASEAN-side agencies like PAGASA and ground disaster-response networks — SaveSignal is proposed as integration, not invention.

Developed for YCAFE 2026

YCAFE — Youth Camp And Friendship's Engagement — brings together youth from Korea and ASEAN member states to build cross-cultural connection and pitch cooperation ideas across the region. SaveSignal was developed by :4-ever as a Korea-ASEAN pitch for the 2026 program, with the Philippines proposed as a pilot region given its exposure to both earthquakes and typhoons, and its existing PAGASA hazard-monitoring infrastructure to build on.
